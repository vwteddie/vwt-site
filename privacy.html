Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026. This is a starting template. Have it reviewed before you rely on it.

Vehicle Wrap Training LLC ("VWT", "we", "us") runs this website. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number and messages when you text, call, email or enroll.
  • Purchase information: checkout runs on Shopify. Shopify and its payment processors handle your payment details. We don't store your card number.
  • Usage information: pages viewed, buttons clicked, device and browser type, and the ad or link that brought you here (for example, UTM parameters).

Cookies and the Meta Pixel

We use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and similar tools. They use cookies to measure how our ads perform and to show our ads to people who may be interested. Meta may combine this with other information it has about you. You can control ad preferences in your Facebook ad settings, and you can block cookies in your browser settings.

How we use it

  • To enroll you, schedule classes and give you support
  • To answer your questions
  • To improve our website and advertising
  • To send class updates and, if you agree, occasional offers. You can opt out at any time.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with services that help us run the business (such as Shopify, Meta, our financing partner when you apply, and email or text tools), or when the law requires it.

Your California privacy rights

California residents can ask what personal information we have about them, ask us to delete it, and opt out of its "sharing" for targeted advertising. To make a request, email us at the address below.
