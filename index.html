Full curriculum

2-Day Window Tint Course

Hand Cutting & Computer Cutting

Learn to cut film by hand and with pattern software and a plotter, then tint a full car on the clock. Here's exactly what each day covers.

  • 2days
  • 6–7hrs / day
  • 4students max
Day 1

Film Knowledge, Prep & Cutting Methods

Learn both ways the pros cut film.

  • Film types: dyed, carbon and ceramic, and what customers are paying for
  • California tint law: legal shades, front windows and windshield strips
  • Tools of the trade: knives, squeegees, heat gun, hard cards and solutions
  • Glass prep: cleaning, scraping and getting the glass contamination-free
  • Hand cutting: cutting patterns on the glass, trimming edges and filing
  • Computer cutting: using pattern software and a plotter to cut precise kits
  • When to hand cut vs. computer cut, and how to pick for speed or custom jobs
  • Side window and roll-down installs

You'll leave Day 1 able to cut and install side windows both ways.

Day 2

Back Glass, Heat Shrinking & a Full Car

Put it all together on a real vehicle.

  • Heat shrinking: forming film to curved back glass
  • Back glass install: dot matrix, defroster lines and clean edges
  • Troubleshooting: dirt, fingers, light gaps, creases and contamination
  • A full car start to finish, on the clock
  • Aftercare and what to tell your customer
  • Pricing tint jobs and getting your first customers

You'll leave Day 2 able to tint a full car and charge for it.

Also included: loaner pro tools in class, a free starter kit, a VWT certificate, lifetime support and the online course and community.

3-Day Clear & Color PPF Course

Clear and color paint protection film, from film basics to a finished panel you install yourself. Here's exactly what each day covers.

  • 3days
  • 6–7hrs / day
  • 4students max
Day 1

Film Knowledge & Application Fundamentals

Understand the film before you touch the car.

  • Clear vs. color PPF: what's different and when to use each
  • Film types and finishes: gloss, matte, satin and color
  • How the film works: top coat, self-healing layer and adhesive
  • Tools of the trade: squeegees, knives, heat gun and solutions
  • Paint prep: wash, decon, clay and wipe-down for a clean install
  • Slip and tack solutions: when and how to use each
  • Squeegee technique on flat panels like the hood and doors

You'll leave Day 1 laying film flat and bubble-free.

Day 2

Film Manipulation, Vehicle Geometry & Troubleshooting

Learn to read the car and plan every panel.

  • Reading vehicle geometry: curves, body lines and recesses
  • Building an install game plan: where to anchor, which way to pull, where to finish
  • Stretching and shrinking film without distortion or lines
  • Managing tension on curved panels like fenders and mirrors
  • Troubleshooting: lift lines, fingers, trapped water, dirt and contamination
  • How to fix a mistake without wasting film

You'll leave Day 2 knowing how to plan any panel before you lay film down.

Day 3

Corners, Permanent Adhesion & Post-Heating

The details that make an install last.

  • Perfecting corners and tight edges
  • Wrapping edges and making clean relief cuts
  • Hiding seams and getting a factory-look finish
  • Post-heating: setting the adhesive so edges don't lift
  • Final inspection, aftercare and what to tell your customer
  • A full panel install start to finish, on your own

You'll leave Day 3 able to deliver an install a customer will pay for.

Also included: loaner pro tools in class, a free starter kit, a VWT certificate, lifetime support and the online course and community.
