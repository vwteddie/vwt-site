## How to run them
- Put 3–4 styles in one ad set and let Meta pick the winner. Good starting mix: **1, 2, 4, 5**.
- Retarget people who visited the landing page with **#6**. On Nov 30, swap it for **Last day**.
- Upload the 4:5 image, then under **Placements → Stories & Reels** swap in the matching 9:16 image.
- **Turn everything off by Nov 30, 11:59 PM PT.**

## Before you launch
- **Permission:** get an OK from everyone pictured before their photo runs as a paid ad.
- **"Reg." prices** must be prices you've actually charged.
- Edit text or prices in `../src/ad2.html` (open with `?ad=1&fmt=feed`, `ad=1..6`, `fmt=story`, `&last=1`).
